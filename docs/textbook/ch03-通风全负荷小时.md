# Chapter 3 — Ventilation Full-Load Hours (Volllaststunden Lüftung)

> Core areas: `Std!A1:V50` (data sheet), `Berechnung LU!K68/K69` (lookup by Regelung), `Lüftung!J7:J22` (Regelung input), `Lüftung!K7:K22` (back-calculated full-load hours)
> Data source: the Raumdatenblätter `Volll_Lüft` sheet (`Std!L2` comment: "Quelle: SIA2024_Raumdatenblätter > tblVoll_Lüft")

## 3.1 Chapter Positioning

The annual electrical energy of the ventilation system's fan $E_{fan} = P_{fan}\cdot t_{VL}$ requires the **full-load hours** (Volllaststunden) $t_{VL}$: converting the varying operating conditions into "$t_{VL}$ hours of operation at rated power". In the Gebäude-Tool, full-load hours appear in three places, each with a different meaning:

1. **Data sheet** (`Std`): the full-load hours snapshot published by SIA 2024 by room use × control mode (einstufig/zweistufig/stufenlos — single-stage/two-stage/continuously variable) × two bases (Volumenstrom / elektrische Energie — volume flow / electrical energy);
2. **Lookup** (`Berechnung LU!K68/K69`): the AHU engine reads $t_{VL,V}$ (volume-flow base) and $t_{VL,E}$ (electrical base) from `Std!Q:V` according to the system's actual control mode (`Lüftung!J`), for the annual energy conversion;
3. **Back-calculation** (`Lüftung!K7` and `Berechnung LU!J7`): the fan electrical energy computed by the AHU ÷ fan power, rounded, yields $t_{VL}$, written back as a display value.

## 3.3 Formula 1 — Definition and Conversion of Full-Load Hours

**Mathematical form**:

$$
t_{VL,E} = \frac{E_{fan,yr}}{P_{fan,nominal}} \quad[\mathrm{h/a}],\qquad
t_{VL,V} = \frac{\int \dot V(t)\,dt}{\dot V_{nominal}} \quad[\mathrm{h/a}]
$$

**Derivation**: the annual fan electrical energy is $E_{fan,yr} = \int P_{fan}(t)\,dt$. If fan power and volume flow are related by $P \propto \dot V^{2.5}$ (see Chapter 4, §4.4), then $t_{VL,E} = \int P\,dt/P_{nom}$ and $t_{VL,V} = \int \dot V\,dt/\dot V_{nom}$ differ: a weighted result of the form $t_{VL,E} = \sum_i f_i\, t_{VL,V,i}$ (the electrical base is more sensitive to low loads). The SIA 2024 data provide both, for use in different applications:
- for the fan **electrical energy** calculation, use $t_{VL,E}$ (`Berechnung LU!K69` looks up columns R/T/V);
- for volume-flow-related **thermal calculations** (e.g. fresh-air load, mixing ratio), use $t_{VL,V}$ (`K68` looks up columns Q/S/U).

**Unit**: h/a.

**Assumption**: rated power = design power (G6); the load-rate distribution follows the hourly operating pattern assumed by SIA 2024 (generated from a 365-day daily model, see Raumdaten `Profile` sheet rows 605–976).

**Scope**: 45 standard room uses; three control-mode levels. **Note**: in `Std`, 12.11 (Kühlraum) is entirely 0, and 12.9 (Parkhaus) has no hygienic fresh air (D47=0) but process air of 2 m³/(h·m²).

## 3.4 Formula 2 — Berechnung LU Lookup by Regelung

**Mathematical form**: the system control mode $r$ (einstufig=1, zweistufig=2, stufenlos=3, `Berechnung LU!I8`) selects the column of the $Std$ sheet:

$$
t_{VL,V} = Std_{r}[\text{col } 2r-1],\qquad t_{VL,E} = Std_{r}[\text{col } 2r]
$$

(For $r=1,2,3$, the $Std$ sheet columns Q..V take columns 1/3/5 and 2/4/6.)

**Workbook implementation** (`Berechnung LU!K68`):

```
=IF($I$8=1, INDEX(Std!$Q$6:$V$50, MATCH('Berechnung LU'!$B$6, Std!$B$6:$B$50, 0), 1),
 IF($I$8=2, INDEX(Std!$Q$6:$V$50, MATCH('Berechnung LU'!$B$6, Std!$B$6:$B$50, 0), 3),
 IF($I$8=3, INDEX(Std!$Q$6:$V$50, MATCH('Berechnung LU'!$B$6, Std!$B$6:$B$50, 0), 5), FALSE)))
```

`K69` takes columns 2/4/6 analogously. `I8` is mapped from `I6` (=`Lüftung!J32`, i.e. the current system's Regelung) via `=IF(I6=Begriffe!F205,1,IF(I6=Begriffe!F206,2,IF(I6=Begriffe!F207,3,FALSE)))` (`Berechnung LU!I8`).

**Unit**: h/a.

**Derivation**: the lookup key is the room-use name `B6` (=`Lüftung!B32`, the use served by the system); `MATCH` locates the row in `Std!B6:B50`; `INDEX` selects the column by control mode. This translates "which control mode did the project select" into "which set of SIA full-load hours to use".

**Assumption**: the project control mode (`Lüftung!J7`) matches the semantics of the control levels defined in the SIA data (einstufig: single speed at rated power all year; zweistufig: two levels 67 %/100 %; stufenlos: continuous variable-speed).

**Verification** (example building LA01, Einzelbüro, einstufig): `K68 = 3900` (`Std!Q10`) ✓; `K69 = 3900` (`Std!R10`) ✓.

**Cell origins**: `Berechnung LU!I6`, `I8`, `K68`, `K69`; `Std!Q6:V50`; `Lüftung!J7:J22` (Regelung input column), `Lüftung!J32` (template row).

## 3.5 Formula 3 — The Closed Loop Between Annual Fan Electrical Energy and Full-Load Hours

**Fan electrical energy** (`Berechnung LU!H7`): `=C259/1000` (C259 is the interval summation column, unit kWh → MWh, see Chapter 4).

**Back-calculated full-load hours** (`Berechnung LU!J7`):

```
=ROUND(IF(G6=0,0, H7*1000/G6), -1)
```

**Mathematical form**: $t_{VL} = \mathrm{round}_{10}\big(E_{fan}\cdot1000 / P_{fan}\big)$, i.e. the annual electrical energy (MWh→kWh) divided by the rated fan power (kW), rounded to the nearest 10 h.

**Analogous formula in the Lüftung sheet** (`Lüftung!K7`): `=IF(H7=0,0,ROUND(I7*1000/H7,-1))`, where `H7 = F7*G7/1000` (volume flow × SFP) and `I7 = 'Berechnung LU'!H7`.

**Unit**: h/a. **Verification**: `H7 = 26.765 MWh`, `G6 = 6.863 kW` → `26.765×1000/6.863 = 3900.0 → 3900` ✓.

**Derivation**: this is the engineering definition of "back-calculating the equivalent full-load time from electrical energy" (as opposed to the lookup value); it agrees with the `K68/K69` lookup values in the example (3900), because the interval-method energy itself is accumulated hour by hour from the operating schedule and $P\propto V^{2.5}$ — the two approaches corroborate each other.

**Assumption**: the rounding of `ROUND(…,-1)` is the specification (the convention of the SIA published values); when G6=0, the output is 0 to prevent division by zero.

**Cell origins**: `Berechnung LU!H7`, `J7`, `C259`; `Lüftung!F7:K7` (and rows 8–22), `Lüftung!I32:J32`.

## 3.6 The Full-Load Hours Usage Chain (Throughout the Document)

```
Lüftung!J7 (Regelung)  ──►  Berechnung LU!I6 ──► I8 (1/2/3)
                                              │
Std!Q:V (SIA 数据) ◄────  Raumdaten Volll_Lüft（副本）
      │
      └──► Berechnung LU!K68 (t_VL,V) ──► 区间年能量 C254…C259
      └──► Berechnung LU!K69 (t_VL,E) ──► 风机/能耗校验 M70, K70
Berechnung LU!H7 (风机电能) ──► Lüftung!I7 ──► Lüftung!K7 = ROUND(I7*1000/H7,-1)
```

- `K70: =E7*K68/8760` (annual volume flow × volume-flow full-load hours ÷ 8760 → a plausibility check in terms of annual average volume flow, m³/h);
- `M70: =G6*K69/K68` (rated power × ratio of electrical/volume-flow full-load hours → equivalent average power);
- `Berechnung LU!I20` (Feuchtelast) also uses `K70`: `=IF(E52=0,0,(E52*1000)/(3600*(K70/3600)*N23))` (moisture load kg/h ÷ (annual average volume flow × density) → g/kg moisture increase).

## 3.7 Porting Notes

1. Full-load hours are **versioned data** (three versions coexist in Raumdaten `Volll_Lüft`: SIA 2024:2015 / prSIA 2024:2021 / prSIA 2024-C1:2024; `Std` is the C1:2024 version snapshot, with `Std!L3` marked "prSIA 2024-C1:2024 (29 m³/h pro Person)"). Ported datasets should carry a version axis.
2. `t_{VL,V}` and `t_{VL,E}` must be preserved in pairs (electrical base ≠ volume-flow base).
3. The rounding of `ROUND(…,-1)` is a specification operation, not floating-point noise.
4. Columns C–I of `Std` (Personenfläche, fresh air, WW) also serve Chapter 2 (volume flow, WW demand); they are three consumers of the same dataset.
